# Formative guidance: IB English A: Language and Literature SL Paper 1

Use this rubric only for text-based practice with a teacher-supplied, non-literary
source. The source must be available during analysis. This is formative feedback,
not an official IB mark or a predicted 1–7 grade.

## JSON-Kriterien

- `interpretation` — coherent interpretation of purpose, audience, and meaning
- `analysis` — explanation of how choices shape meaning and reader response
- `evidence` — precise, accurate details or short quotations from the source
- `organization` — focused line of analysis and purposeful paragraphing
- `language` — clear and accurate analytical writing
- `register` — appropriate formal analytical register

## Criteria in detail

- `interpretation` — Presents a coherent interpretation of the text's purpose,
  audience, and central meaning. Do not reward unsupported claims.
- `analysis` — Explains how the writer's choices (such as diction, tone,
  structure, register, or persuasive techniques) shape meaning and reader response.
  Feature-spotting without explanation is not analysis.
- `evidence` — Uses precise, relevant details or short quotations from the
  supplied source and explains their significance. Never credit invented or
  inaccurate quotations.
- `organization` — Develops a focused line of analysis with clear, purposeful
  paragraphing and links between claims and evidence.
- `language` — Communicates ideas clearly and accurately, with vocabulary and
  sentence choices suited to analytical writing.
- `register` — Maintains an appropriate formal analytical register without
  formulaic claims or unsupported certainty.

## Feedback rules

Refer to the supplied source and the student's actual wording. Quote only exact
short phrases from those inputs. Distinguish evidence from interpretation, name
what is working, and give one concrete next step. If the source or task is
missing, flag that limitation. Never infer author intention beyond what the
text supports, and never output an overall numeric grade.
